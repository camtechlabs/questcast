QuestCast Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

QuestCast ("the app") is developed by Cameron Barry ("we", "us"). This policy covers both the QuestCast app for Meta Quest and the QuestCast Phone app for Android.

We collect nothing

We collect nothing. QuestCast does not collect, store, transmit to us, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

How the app works

How the app works: the two apps communicate directly with each other over your local Wi-Fi network. Your phone's screen video and your touch input travel only between your own phone and your own headset on your own network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server, and no recordings are kept.

What stays on your devices

What stays on your devices: the only thing either app stores is your own pairing preference — the name and local network address of your phone, kept on the headset so it can reconnect automatically. It never leaves the headset.

Data deletion

Data deletion: because QuestCast collects and stores no personal data, there is no user data held by us to delete. You can remove the pairing preference described above at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you believe we hold any information about you, or you have any other deletion request, contact us at twicethecam@gmail.com and we will respond.

Permissions (Android companion app)

Permissions (Android companion app): Screen capture (MediaProjection) is used only while you are mirroring, only after you accept Android's consent prompt each session. The Accessibility Service is used only to perform the taps and swipes you make on the Quest panel, on your phone. Network access is used only to find and connect to your Quest on your local network.

Permissions (Quest app)

Permissions (Quest app): Network and Wi-Fi state access are used only to find and connect to your phone on your local network.

Children

Children: the app is a general utility and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
